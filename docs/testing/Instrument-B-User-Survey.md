## Scoring

Compute the mean per statement and per category. Interpret with:

| Mean | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| 4.21 – 5.00 | Strongly Agree / Excellent |
| 3.41 – 4.20 | Agree / Very Good |
| 2.61 – 3.40 | Neutral / Good |
| 1.81 – 2.60 | Disagree / Fair |
| 1.00 – 1.80 | Strongly Disagree / Poor |

Present the results in a table in the final report and explain what they mean. Do not fill in results before real respondents have answered.
