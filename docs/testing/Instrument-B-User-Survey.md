# Instrument B — User Evaluation Survey

Answered by the client and real users after they try the system (Week 16). At least **[10]** respondents including the client; the number is unconfirmed (D-03), and who takes part is open (D-41).

**Status:** respondents planned (see Participants; working plan of Nov 25, 2026, decision 106). **The 12 statements are still not in this repository and have not been written.** Copy them word for word from the Final Project Guide. Do not invent or paraphrase them.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

## Participants

Working plan (decision 106, D-41). The client's names and the dates are still to be confirmed; the plan is not a client approval.

| Respondent | Who |

- **10 respondents**, the Guide's bracketed minimum (D-03). Fewer only with the instructor's agreement.
- **The group never answers.** Its members built the system.
- **When:** Week 16, after the training session, on the day of the try-out or the next day.
- **How:** each person first uses the system with **fictional data** on the test computer (sign in, find a resident, issue and print a document, open a report, sign out), then fills in their own sheet alone, without discussing the answers. The group only helps if the person is stuck, and notes it.
- Record the role and the date, never the person's name unless the barangay agrees (codes below).

## Scoring

Compute the mean per statement and per category. Interpret with:

| Mean | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| 4.21 – 5.00 | Strongly Agree / Excellent |
| 3.41 – 4.20 | Agree / Very Good |
| 2.61 – 3.40 | Neutral / Good |
| 1.81 – 2.60 | Disagree / Fair |
| 1.00 – 1.80 | Strongly Disagree / Poor |

Present the results in a table in the final report and explain what they mean. Do not fill in results before real respondents have answered.
