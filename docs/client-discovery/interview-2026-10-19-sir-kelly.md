# Client Interview: October 19, 2026

## Interview details

- **Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Location:** Barangay Mayondon Hall
- **Interview participant:** Sir Kelly Renz Villegas
- **Purpose:** Understand current record management and document-processing problems
- **Record type:** Team-prepared summary of interview notes

## Current workflow

The barangay staff use Google Sheets to maintain resident, family, census, beneficiary, and ID-related masterlists. They also use Excel templates to prepare and print barangay documents, replacing the details in a template for each new request.

## Problems identified

- Multiple spreadsheet copies can make shared records difficult to manage.
- Staff reported saving errors and missing files.
- Preparing a new document can require repeated entry and correction of details.
- Reusing a template does not provide an organized history of each previously issued document.
- Staff need to search for existing resident information when a person returns for another document.

## Initial needs identified

- Search and retrieve existing resident records.
- Reduce repeated data entry when preparing documents.
- Preserve a separate record of document requests and issuances.
- Consider an offline-first setup because internet connectivity is slow.

## Materials and follow-up

The team discussed sample barangay documents. The materials inventory records the reference documents and files listed in the team's Creative Brief. Verify the exact receipt and access status of each file before relying on it.

A follow-up interview was requested to clarify document types, Barangay ID preparation, record fields, and the local deployment setup.
